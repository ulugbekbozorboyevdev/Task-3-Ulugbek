LEGITIMATE MESSAGE 1
--------------------
Subject: Meeting Reminder

Hello,

This is a reminder that our study group meeting is scheduled for Monday at
10:00 AM in Room 204. Please bring your notes.

Why it appears legitimate:
- Gives ordinary meeting information.
- Does not request passwords or payment.
- Does not pressure the recipient to click a link.
- The message is consistent with an expected school activity.


LEGITIMATE MESSAGE 2
--------------------
Subject: Assignment Submission Confirmation

Hello,

Your assignment has been received successfully. You can check its status by
opening the school platform through your usual bookmark or official app.

Thank you.

Why it appears legitimate:
- Confirms an expected activity.
- Does not ask for sensitive information.
- Does not include an unfamiliar link or unexpected attachment.
- Suggests using the usual official platform.

Note:
These are fictional examples for educational practice. A message should not be
considered safe based only on its wording; always check its context and sender.
