PHISHING MESSAGE 1
------------------
Subject: Urgent: Account Suspended

URGENT! Your account has been suspended. Verify your account immediately:
http://account-check.example/login

Red flags:
- Uses urgent language to pressure the recipient.
- Claims the account is suspended.
- Asks the recipient to verify their account through an unexpected link.
- Uses HTTP instead of HTTPS.

Why it is suspicious:
The message creates pressure and directs the recipient to an unfamiliar website.
The link uses the reserved example domain and is included only for demonstration.


PHISHING MESSAGE 2
------------------
Subject: You Have Won a Prize!

Congratulations! You have won a special prize.
Claim your reward now:
https://bit.ly/sample-prize

Red flags:
- Announces an unexpected prize.
- Encourages the recipient to act quickly.
- Uses a shortened URL that hides the final destination.

Why it is suspicious:
Unexpected prize messages can be used to attract clicks. A shortened link makes
it harder to see where the recipient will be sent. A short link is not proof of
phishing by itself.


PHISHING MESSAGE 3
------------------
Subject: Verify Your Password

Dear Customer,

We noticed unusual activity. Please verify your account password to avoid
account suspension:
https://secure-login.example/verify

Red flags:
- Uses a generic greeting.
- Requests password-related action.
- Threatens account suspension.
- Directs the recipient to an unfamiliar domain.

Why it is suspicious:
Legitimate services should not ask users to disclose their passwords through
an unexpected message. The recipient should visit the official website or app
directly instead of using the message link.
