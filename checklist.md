# Phishing Red Flag Checklist

Use this checklist to identify potentially suspicious emails and messages.

## 10 Common Phishing Red Flags

- [ ] **Unknown sender:** The message comes from an unfamiliar or suspicious email address.
- [ ] **Urgent language:** It pressures you to act immediately.
- [ ] **Password requests:** It asks you to share your password or login credentials.
- [ ] **Unexpected attachments:** It includes files you were not expecting.
- [ ] **Suspicious links:** Links lead to unfamiliar or unexpected websites.
- [ ] **Misspelled domains:** Website addresses contain spelling mistakes or unusual variations of known domains.
- [ ] **Unexpected prizes:** It claims you have won a prize or reward you never entered for.
- [ ] **Unusual payment requests:** It asks for unexpected payments or financial information.
- [ ] **Generic greetings:** It uses greetings such as "Dear Customer" instead of addressing you personally.
- [ ] **Fear and pressure:** It threatens account suspension or other negative consequences unless you act quickly.

## What Should You Do?

1. Do not click suspicious links or open unexpected attachments.
2. Never share passwords or verification codes.
3. Verify the sender through an official communication channel.
4. Report suspicious messages to the appropriate organization or security team.

**Remember:** One red flag does not automatically prove that a message is phishing. Consider the overall context before deciding.
